## Pages without seeds

These pages have no seeded issue of their own and should produce no page findings: /features, /blog/peak-hours, /help, /signin, /app/schedule, /app/team, /app/team/meera-iyer, /app/time-off, /app/billing, /app/notifications. They all load the shared stylesheets, so the design-system seeds (S-11 to S-15) can still be reported against them. Their charts are meant to be accessible: role="img" with a name that states the finding, a caption or a data table, series told apart by text or line style, and series colours of at least 3:1.
