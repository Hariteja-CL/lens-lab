Staffing the morning rush without overstaffing the afternoon

4 November 2026 · 5 minute read

Most cafés know their busy hours by feel. Writing them down as numbers makes the rota easier to defend and easier to change.

Start with covers per hour

Export a month of till receipts and count how many orders start in each hour. Here is a typical weekday at a fictional 40-seat café.

Turn covers into cover targets

As a starting point, plan one barista for every 20 orders an hour and one person on the floor for every 30. Adjust after two weeks.

Check it every month

Menus, seasons and school terms all move the rush. A monthly look at the numbers keeps the rota honest.
