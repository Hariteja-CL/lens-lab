Everything a small café needs to run its rota

Fernhollow is built for teams of 5 to 60 people who work early starts, split shifts and busy weekends.

Rota builder

Set the cover you need hour by hour. Fernhollow suggests a rota that respects availability, rest breaks and contracted hours, and highlights any gaps in amber before you publish.

Availability and time off

Staff set their availability and request leave from their phones. Managers approve in one place, and approved leave is blocked out of the rota automatically.

Swap requests

When someone cannot make a shift, Fernhollow offers it to qualified teammates who are free, and tells the manager once it is covered.

Reports that match the till

Compare planned hours with worked hours and sales, so you can staff the rush without paying for quiet afternoons.
