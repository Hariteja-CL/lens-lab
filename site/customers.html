Cafés that plan with Fernhollow

Three fictional cafés, three different problems. All names and numbers are invented.

Kesari Crumb, Pune

A bakery café with 14 staff and a 6 am opening. The owner used to build the rota by hand every Sunday.

“Sunday evenings used to disappear into a spreadsheet. Now the rota is done before my coffee is.”

Nisha Varma, owner

Result: rota time down from 3 hours to 25 minutes a week.

Bluepine Brew Room, Shillong

Two sites and a lot of weekend overtime. Fernhollow's cover targets showed where extra people were booked on quiet afternoons.

Overtime hours per month, before and after Fernhollow

“We stopped guessing. The rush is covered and the quiet hours are not.”

Daniel Lyngdoh, operations lead

Saffron Steps Coffee, Jaipur

Students on changing timetables. Availability updates from phones replaced a wall calendar and a group chat.

“Swaps happen in minutes, and I only hear about them when they are done.”

Ritika Shekhawat, manager

Result: 9 in 10 swap requests filled without the manager stepping in.

Fernhollow is a fictional product made to demonstrate CraftLayers Lens. Any resemblance to a real product or company is unintended.
