Frequently asked questions

How do I add a new team member?

Go to Team, choose Invite a teammate, enter their work email and role. They get an invitation by email.

Can staff see each other's phone numbers?

No. Staff see names and shifts only. Managers can see contact details.

What happens when I publish a rota?

Everyone on it gets a notification with their shifts for the week. Later changes are sent as updates.

Still stuck?

Email the demo support team at help@fernhollow.example.test. This is a fictional address.
