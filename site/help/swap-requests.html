How swap requests work

Help centre · Updated 10 November 2026

A swap request lets someone hand a shift to a teammate without the manager rearranging the rota by hand.

Offer a shift

  1. Open the shift on your phone or in the Schedule.
  2. Choose Offer this shift and add a short note if you like.
  3. Fernhollow sends the offer to teammates who have the right role and are free at that time.

Accept a shift

Choose Take this shift on the offer. The first qualified person to accept gets it, and the rota updates for everyone.

What managers see

Managers get one notification when the swap is complete. If nobody accepts within 12 hours, the manager is asked to step in.

Limits

  • Swaps cannot break rest-break rules or push someone over their contracted hours.
  • Shifts that start in less than two hours go straight to the manager.
